# Project status and roadmap

## Working MVP

- Homeowner profiles and guided interview
- Dependency-aware retrofit scenarios
- Individual, One Stop Shop and Fully Funded route comparison
- SEAI median costs, published grants and net-if-approved calculations
- Apartments, private applicants and Approved Housing Bodies
- Manual BER evidence, synthetic fixtures and bounded text-PDF certificate extraction
- Evidence-gated illustrative BER potential ranges
- Versioned contractor PDF briefs
- Access controls, consent, configurable retention, deletion requests and audit events
- Dated public-source snapshots with keyword and vector retrieval adapters

## Next validation gates

1. Have a qualified energy adviser review sequencing, safety dependencies and grant interpretation.
2. Run credentialed OpenAI response and embedding smoke tests.
3. Establish an authorised, dependable SEAI source-refresh arrangement.
4. Exercise PostgreSQL and pgvector on Cloud SQL and verify cross-instance leases.
5. Complete privacy, security, backup and deletion-replay reviews.
6. Pilot with consenting homeowners and advisers using agreed success measures.

## Later opportunities

- Authorised BER data integration
- OCR with confidence scoring and homeowner or assessor confirmation
- Managed identity, MFA and account recovery
- Private object storage for reports at larger scale
- Partner workflows for advisers, contractors, One Stop Shops, lenders and Approved Housing Bodies

Roadmap items are proposals, not committed integrations or partnerships.
